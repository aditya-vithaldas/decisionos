Workspace privacy

Your own Google identity and mailbox. No outgoing message without explicit approval.

Google identity and Gmail

Verified identity separates each user's workspace. OAuth requests Gmail read and send permissions. One account-scoped fourteen-day source fetch serves all five tabs, excluding Spam and Trash. Sales, actions and finance analyze its seven-day subset; clustering uses fourteen days. Job hunting preserves older grounded records but does not automatically repeat a 180-day search. Older historical retrieval may be incomplete. These are windowed views, not a complete mailbox audit. Fetch can be stopped and continued. While a signed-in page is visible, incremental source checks run approximately every five minutes with overlap protection and retry backoff; they do not run models or classify automatically. Workspace labels never move or delete Gmail messages.

AI processing

Analyze Mail uses TypeSafe AI's JEV on stored subjects and short excerpts; sender addresses and full threads are not included. Analyze does not call Google Gemini, discover topics automatically or refetch Gmail. Starter topics are used for clustering. Actual probabilities do not guarantee correctness. Uncertain potentially relevant items remain for review; another topic is hidden from that lens, not deleted. Unavailable providers produce an error, never substitute scores. Explicit brief reply generation can send bounded source context and your answers to Google Gemini. Email contents remain untrusted data, never executable instructions.

Live voice

Clicking Live and granting microphone permission starts audio streaming to Google Gemini 3.8 Live for transcription, audio responses and explicit brief drafts. Current visible card titles and identifiers are included as selection context, not your whole mailbox. Only after an explicit Reply request, a bounded excerpt of the selected source email and your guidance are also supplied to Live to prepare a concise natural draft; exact-message dictation is not required. Local ordinal/title selection avoids model decisions; ambiguous semantic selection can send bounded title context to JEV. Server-constrained short-lived tokens keep the long-lived provider key private. Stop, navigation or leaving the page closes microphone tracks. A folded typed-command control works without capturing audio. Voice cannot send mail automatically; review and explicitly approve any outgoing draft. Do not enable Live if you do not want microphone audio processed by Google.

Storage and display

Encrypted OAuth tokens, private prompts, source snapshots, summaries, statuses and reviewed draft/send records are stored under your account in Google Cloud Firestore. Fetch makes no model calls. Changed versions are appended to encrypted snapshots, with active shared source references bounded to fourteen days. Analyze consumes these snapshots and actual classifications are cached. Snapshot access expires after an hour, not physical deletion; older saved records require an owner deletion request. Expanded conversations are fetched only when requested and displayed as plain text without tracking images or scripts. Payment-failure cards show only literal source amounts, references, dates and action instructions when present; missing facts remain unknown. No payment or retry is executed. Google and TypeSafe process data under their applicable terms. We do not sell mailbox data or train our own models on it.

Sending and your controls

Review the exact recipient, subject and editable body, then click Send this reply to approve that message. Opening Reply, answering questions or generating a draft never sends. Sending is once-only; uncertain sends are not automatically retried. Done, Not important and Discard hide the exact seen message version across every tab, with Undo, without changing Gmail. A genuinely newer message can reappear. Disconnect revokes stored access; sign out ends the session. You can also revoke access in Google. Saved records remain until removed; request deletion through Contact Us. Do not connect sensitive mail you do not want processed by the named services.
