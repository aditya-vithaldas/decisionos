Visible capture.
Explicit consent.

Participants separately choose microphone, screen/tab, camera, and face-cue estimates. Nothing starts recording before explicit consent and Start. Browser permission denial is respected. Stop ends all device capture.

What is processed

The selected audio, tab/screen and camera are stored privately in Google Cloud Storage. Google Cloud Speech-to-Text processes microphone audio in approximately 20-second chunks, in English. This is near-live transcription, not a sub-second streaming service. Face-movement estimates use Google's MediaPipe model locally; saved coefficients indicate approximate visible movements, not emotions, intent, identity, or mental state. Hume is not configured or claimed.

Reports and access

The study owner signs in with Google. Only that owner can retrieve study reports and recordings. The unguessable participant link lets its holders read the task and begin a session, not see other participants or reports. Each participant receives a separate session token. Timestamped typed notes, recognized speech and context/cue events are saved in Firestore. Transcripts can be wrong; statements and inferred triage categories are distinguished. No hidden cross-origin page inspection occurs.

Embedding and browsing

Sites may block iframe previews with security headers. We do not bypass these protections. Use a normal tab and explicitly select it for sharing. Cross-origin clicks and URLs cannot be read by this app; selected-tab video and participant context provide navigation evidence. Avoid passwords, payment details, confidential pages and bystanders. Optional camera can remain off.

Limits and retention

Ten sessions per study, six minutes per session, 100 MB captured media per session, and seven-day access. Private recordings have a seven-day cloud deletion lifecycle, which is asynchronous. Study/session metadata is configured for seven-day TTL cleanup, also asynchronous. Owner can disable a study link. Ask for deletion through Contact Us. No real device recording was used in automated validation; fixtures test capture/provider handling.

Provider costs

Google speech recognition, Cloud Run, Firestore and storage usage may incur normal project charges. No claim is made that this is the cheapest provider.
