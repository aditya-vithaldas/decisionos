# Independent Asana product exploration

Public article: /projects/asana/; prototype: /projects/asana/prototype/.

All prototype data and concept screenshots are fictional. No original logged-in Asana account captures are published. This is an independent exploration, not commissioned by or affiliated with Asana. Existing capabilities are distinguished from proposed ones in the article.

The final film is 60 seconds at 1920×1080, with six scenes, including an 18-second dashboard sequence and 12-second meeting and Gantt sequences, generated editorial illustrations and original instrumental music; no narration or company logos. Illustration sources were generated with the image generation tool, then edited to show project-plan changes, Gantt charts and assigned tasks. The instrumental score was procedurally composed for this film (80 BPM; warm pads, plucked keys, soft bass and light percussion), with no sampled recordings or third-party music. Local editable production sources remain in /Users/aditya/programming/Teardowns/Asana/video/marketing-v8/.

The prototype is browser-only. It does not connect accounts, access microphones, send follow-ups, join meetings, or start coding sessions.
