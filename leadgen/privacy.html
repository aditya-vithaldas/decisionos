Lead generator privacy

No sign-in is required. Enter a public business website to find questions your business can help answer.

What we use

Your website, inferred business offering and recommended questions are sent to Google Gemini and Google Search to research public content. This public flow does not save your company profile or results to an account or database. Results remain in this browser page until you refresh or leave. Hosting infrastructure may retain standard request logs. A temporary hashed network address is used for rate limits.

Public discovery

Coverage is limited to publicly discoverable content. We do not access private social accounts. Questions, product fit and reply-status labels are AI suggestions based on cited search evidence. Read the original conversation to verify context and current replies.

Your replies

Opening a conversation takes you to the original platform, which may require its own account. You write and submit replies there; the lead generator does not post on your behalf.
