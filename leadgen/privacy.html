Lead generator privacy

Google sign-in verifies your identity. This service requests identity permissions only. It does not access your Gmail, Google Sheets, or private social accounts.

What we use and store

Your company name, website, offering and selected questions are sent to Google Gemini and Google Search to research public content. Your company profile, sourced research, latest results and lead statuses are encrypted in the existing account-scoped Decision Axis storage. Google account name and email are used to identify your workspace. Search suggestions supplied by Google are displayed in a sandboxed frame.

Public discovery

Coverage is limited to publicly discoverable content. This service does not log into LinkedIn, Reddit or X on your behalf and cannot promise complete coverage, accurate dates or an active buying opportunity. Cards show AI summaries with source citations; read the original post to verify context.

Your replies

Opening a conversation takes you to the original platform. You write and submit replies there. Saved, dismissed and replied statuses only change your workspace. Marking a lead replied is your own record, not verification that a reply was posted.

Example mode

The Decision Axis example contains illustrative questions only, with no invented prospects or conversations. It performs no research and is not saved to your account.

Account controls

Sign out ends your session. To request removal of your stored lead generator data, contact aditya@decisionos.me.
