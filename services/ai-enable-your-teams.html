AI-enable your teams.

Build the workflows, context and skills that make AI useful in everyday work.

Giving a team access to AI is a starting point. The useful work is helping people apply it to the decisions, tasks and handoffs they deal with every day. Decision Axis connects that change to the way your team already works.

Start with a real workflow.

Choose a recurring piece of work with a clear owner and an outcome you can judge. It might be turning customer interviews into a product decision, preparing a useful account brief, investigating a business question, or taking a product idea into a prototype.

We look at the current process together: the inputs, the judgment involved, the tools people use, and where work stalls. Then we define a focused first use case and what a good result would look like.

Make the right context available.

Useful AI work depends on context: the product brief, customer evidence, terminology, quality standards, examples and constraints that your team relies on. We help organise that material so people and agents can use it consistently.

The output can be a small maintained knowledge base, a set of working templates, or a connection to the systems the team already uses. Each source needs an owner and a way to stay current.

Decide where AI should act.

Some work benefits from a draft. Some needs a recommendation with supporting evidence. Other tasks can run through to completion once the scope and permissions are clear.

We work through those choices explicitly. What can the agent read? What can it change? When should a person review the result? What happens when information is missing or the result is uncertain? Those decisions become part of the workflow.

Learn by doing the work.

Training is most useful when people bring their own examples. We work through real tasks, compare results, and develop a shared sense of what to trust, what to check, and how to improve an instruction or a workflow.

Product managers, designers, engineers and operational teams need different examples. The sessions and supporting material follow the work each group needs to do.

Run a focused pilot and improve it.

Try the workflow with a small group. Review the quality of the output, the effort needed to correct it, the time spent across the whole process, and the actual operating cost. Use that evidence to decide what to improve and what to expand.

A useful handover includes working examples, clear ownership, a short playbook, and a way to review failures. The aim is a team that can keep improving its use of AI as its tools and needs change.

How Decision Axis can help.

An engagement can combine workflow discovery, practical workshops, hands-on setup, pilot delivery and coaching. We agree the scope around the work you want to improve and the support your team needs.
