Organizational design · Operating models

Give every team a clear job to do.

Organizational structure advisory for founders and product leaders facing unclear ownership, slow decisions and too many handoffs.

Discuss your team structure ↗

Start where the work gets stuck.

A reporting line alone does not explain who owns an outcome. DecisionAxis works with you to understand how product, design, engineering and business teams make decisions and get work done.

Bring a concrete problem: priorities that keep changing, teams waiting on each other, decisions returning to the founder, or responsibilities that overlap as the organization grows. We use those situations to define the scope of the advisory work.

Structure around outcomes and decisions.

  • Team boundaries: clarify the customer or business outcome each team owns and the dependencies it needs to manage.
  • Decision rights: agree who decides, who contributes and when an issue needs escalation.
  • Operating model: connect goals, planning, delivery and review through a small set of useful practices.
  • AI responsibilities: where relevant, define ownership of agent workflows, evaluation and human review.

What you can leave with.

Depending on the agreed scope, the deliverables can include a current-state assessment, a proposed team structure, an ownership and decision map, and a transition plan. We make the tradeoffs explicit so you can choose what fits your organization.

Start with discovery and a working session. Develop the design with the people who will use it, then review the proposed changes against real decisions and handoffs before expanding them.

Who you’ll work with.

Aditya Vithaldas, a Berlin-based product thinker and hands-on builder with experience across product strategy, design and engineering. The engagement connects organizational choices to the product work your teams need to deliver.

Questions before you start

Do we need a full reorganization?

The scope can focus on one team, a recurring handoff or a specific set of decisions. Discovery helps establish whether a structural change is needed.

Is this product team design or company-wide advisory?

The focus is product organizations and the teams they work with. We agree the organizational boundary and stakeholder involvement before starting.

Can this include our move to AI agents?

Yes. Team design can include responsibility for agent behavior, permissions, evaluation, escalation and ongoing maintenance. If you also need a build, explore AI agent integration.
