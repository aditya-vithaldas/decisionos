Quiet pages, one blue dot.

One narrow column of small, calm type. Near-black on white, with grey for everything secondary. Blue appears in two places only: links, and the single dot in every illustration that marks the decision.

Principles

  • One column, 600px wide. Nothing sits beside it except on the onboarding tool.
  • Small type, generous space. Hierarchy comes from spacing and grey, not from size.
  • Real content only: real roles, real products, real articles. No filler, no invented numbers.
  • One illustration per page, near the end or beside a tool, never as decoration on every block.
  • Plain words. Short sentences. Figures as numerals.

Illustration style

Every illustration is one continuous line of fine black ink on pure white, with exactly one small solid blue dot marking the point of decision. The subject is small inside a lot of empty space: people at work, paths, forks and rising arrows.

Do

  • One thin, even, unbroken line; it may loop once to join shapes.
  • One blue dot (#204dff), small, on the moment of choice: the fork, the screen, the next step.
  • Pure white ground, at least two-thirds of the frame empty.
  • Raster PNG, cleaned to pure white, 1200px wide for wide images and 800px for spots.

Don’t

  • No text, shading, hatching, fills, gradients, borders or paper texture.
  • No second colour, and never more than one blue dot.
  • No vector or hand-coded SVG drawings; generate raster images.

Voice

  • Say what it is, then what it does for the reader: “AI agent integration · In your product, or in ChatGPT via MCP”.
  • Name real things: companies, products, years. Prefer a fact over an adjective.
  • Sentence case everywhere. No exclamation marks.
